How it works

  1. Every night, a script pulls every liquid prediction market and ranks what actually moved.
  2. Near-resolved markets are dropped. If a market is already at 97%, it isn't news.
  3. One market per event. You get signal, not eight variations of the same game.
  4. A human writes the voice. The data is machine-collected; the judgement and the jokes are not.

Odds are market prices, not probabilities. We don't give financial advice and we don't tell you what to bet on. If that's what you're after, this isn't for you.
